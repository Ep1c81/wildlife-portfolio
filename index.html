About Me

With over 5 years of field experience in wildlife rescue, clinical rehabilitation, and habitat management, my mission is to foster ecological coexistence and restore native fauna to their natural environments.

I specialize in raptor and mammal intake assessment, emergency stabilization, diet design, and public education campaigns aimed at reducing human-wildlife conflict.
